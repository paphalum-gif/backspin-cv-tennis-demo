Eligibility

Backspin Tournament is for amateur tennis players. Players should review their eligibility before registering.

  • Backspin Champion status may affect eligibility for the same discipline, category and level.

  • Singles and Doubles are treated as separate disciplines.

Rules & Regulations

Round Robin · 1 set / 6 games. At 5–5, play a 7-point tiebreak. Deuce is Sudden Death / No-Ad. Check in at least 1 hour before your match. A 15-minute late arrival is a forfeit. Organizer / umpire decision is final.
